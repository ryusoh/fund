## Executive summary

**Classification: Bullish for near-term execution; neutral-to-bearish for valuation.** Q1 materially strengthened the FY2027 execution case: revenue was **$33.007M**, bookings **$46.7M**, quarter-end backlog **$100.9M**, and effective backlog **$109.3M**. GAAP/non-GAAP gross margin recovered to **47.5%/48.5%**, and FY2027 revenue guidance moved from **$130–150M to $150M**, with non-GAAP net income now expected to be **at least 20% of revenue** (Q1-ER pp. 1–2).

The print clears the thesis's Q1 revenue kill test and weakens the immediate backlog-evaporation Bear branch. It does **not** establish the durable FY2031 Bull case: the second AI supplier has only **“decided to purchase”** one FOX-XP evaluation cell, the second Sonoma processor belongs to the **same lead hyperscale customer**, customer-level Q1 revenue is undisclosed, and operating cash flow was **negative $3.054M** despite $9.357M of non-GAAP net income (Q1-ER pp. 2, 5–7).

Recommended scenario response: move five percentage points from Bear to Base (**Bull/Base/Bear 0.25/0.50/0.25**, from 0.25/0.45/0.30), keep Bull probability, EPS CAGRs, and exit P/Es unchanged, and rebase starting EPS to a conservative **$0.895** calculated from guided minimum non-GAAP net income of $30M divided by Q1 diluted weighted shares of 33.520M. At the pipeline's 2026-11-03 price of $64.52, the five-period engine produces **1.3060× expected multiple and 5.4849% expected CAGR**, below both the 6.5% benchmark and 12% hurdle. Kelly remains zero; the calculated 12%-hurdle entry price is **$47.81**.

### Guidance and required ramp

- Current FY2027 guidance: **$150M revenue and non-GAAP net income of at least 20% of revenue**. Prior guidance: **$130–150M and 18–22%** (Q1-ER p. 2; Q4-ER p. 3).
- The new guide implies at least **$30M** non-GAAP net income. It is a move to the former revenue high end, not a new range above $150M.
- Derived remaining requirement: **$150.000 − $33.007 = $116.993M** over Q2–Q4, an average **$38.998M per quarter**, **18.1% above Q1**. Q1 annualization alone would not reach the revised guide.
- Starting-EPS model input: **$30M / 33.520M = $0.894988**, rounded to **$0.895**. This is a conservative floor-case calculation, **not company-issued EPS guidance**; future diluted shares may differ.

### Customer programs and concentration

- The benchmarked major AI supplier **“has decided to purchase”** a fully configured FOX-XP WLBI test cell for evaluation in a high-volume-production environment at a key Taiwan OSAT. Installation is planned for early CY2027, with an HVM decision anticipated within several months after installation and validation. This is not yet an HVM award (Q1-ER p. 2).
- Aehr is shipping and installing **more than a dozen Sonoma systems per month** for the lead hyperscaler's first AI processor. The **same customer's** second processor has completed Sonoma qualification and has follow-on orders for initial sampling and an anticipated CY2027 ramp. The customer's forecast of higher ultimate volume is non-binding (Q1-ER pp. 2–3).
- Management says more than a dozen customers now have the **potential** to generate significant production orders. No Q1 customer identities, customer-level revenue percentages, product revenue split, or geographic split are disclosed. FY2026 concentration remains the latest full-year actual: top customer 26.3%, top five approximately 70% (FY26-10K Item 1).
- Most customer purchase orders are cancellable or reschedulable with limited penalties, and backlog is not necessarily indicative of future sales (FY26-10K Item 1A). Effective backlog is therefore not contracted FY2027 revenue.

## Red-team case — preserved independently

The strongest adversarial conclusion is that Q1 validates a **one-year equipment ramp**, not yet a **multi-year, diversified, cash-generative annuity**. Disconfirming evidence, ranked by thesis-killing potential:

1. **Concentration may be rising, not falling.** The second Sonoma processor increases exposure to the same lead hyperscaler. “More than a dozen potential customers” supplies no measured revenue diversification. A pause or insourcing decision at one account could affect two processor generations, systems, consumables, backlog, receivables, and inventory simultaneously.
2. **Valuation still dominates the investment outcome.** Updating the EPS base and modestly reweighting scenarios yields only 5.4849% expected CAGR at $64.52. A strong print does not by itself justify a higher terminal multiple or Bull probability.
3. **Backlog coverage is optical, not contractual.** Effective backlog covers 93.4% of the remaining revenue requirement arithmetically, but includes post-quarter orders, has no fiscal-quarter delivery schedule, and is largely cancellable/reschedulable.
4. **Earnings did not convert to cash.** Receivables and inventory consumed $19.723M combined. Rapid growth legitimately consumes working capital, but long-lead inventory and customer receivables increase loss asymmetry if orders slip.
5. **The second AI supplier is not a production win.** One evaluation cell supplies no multi-system PO, committed wafer volume, validated uptime/throughput, or production share.
6. **Recurring consumables remain unverified for this quarter.** The third-party transcript's $13.5M/41% Q1 consumables claim is absent from official materials. Even if later confirmed, initial fills must be distinguished from replacement and utilization-driven recurrence.
7. **Non-GAAP headline exceeds owner economics.** Q1 excluded $1.880M of stock compensation and included $0.899M of net interest income plus a $0.254M tax benefit. Do not treat the 28.3% quarterly non-GAAP net margin as a steady-state margin.
8. **Dilution remains a per-share modeling issue.** Q1 shares outstanding rose 0.56% from FYE, diluted shares exceeded basic shares, and historical cash was strengthened by equity issuance. Optionality cannot be assumed to offset dilution automatically.

### Evidence that would flip the scenarios

- **Toward Bull:** second-supplier multi-system/HVM order by 2027-06-30; FY2027 revenue ≥$150M and non-GAAP net income ≥$30M; FY2028 guidance consistent with the Bull growth path; top customer ≤30%; demonstrable cash conversion.
- **Toward Bear:** official FY2027 guide cut; nine-month revenue below ~$105M without identified Q4 coverage; lead-customer pause, insourcing, credible competing WLBI qualification, or reduced burn-in intensity; persistent negative operating cash flow despite guided profits.
- **Bear falsifier:** FY2027 revenue and non-GAAP income meet the revised guide while operating cash flow reaches at least ~70% of guided minimum non-GAAP income and no new equity offering occurs.
